## What changed & why

- Replaced the bug‑prone cross‑sectional quality percentile approach with a **static, multi‑factor quality sub‑score** (`_quality_score`) that directly combines ROE, ROA, gross margin, current ratio, inverse debt‑to‑equity, and FCF/total assets.
- Removed the (often‑broken) `_compute_quality_raw` method and the percentile recalculation in `rank()`, eliminating the `pe_safe` NameError and any dependence on cross‑sectional rankings.
- The composite is now computed **once** inside `score()` using the final quality score, making the scorer robust and simpler while focusing quality on proven fundamentals that should associate with future returns without the instability of ranking relative to the current universe.
