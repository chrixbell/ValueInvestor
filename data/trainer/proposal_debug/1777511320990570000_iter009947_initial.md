Replaced the multiplicative blend `(score_fcf * score_cr) / 100` with the geometric mean `math.sqrt(score_fcf * score_cr)`. The product penalizes stocks too harshly when one financial-strength dimension is modest, often producing near‑zero momentum scores. The geometric mean spreads the distribution more evenly while still rewarding consistent strength, which may improve rank correlation with forward returns.
