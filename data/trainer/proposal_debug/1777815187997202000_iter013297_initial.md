### What changed and why

The growth raw metric used for cross‑sectional ranking was changed from plain inverse PEG (`1 / PEG`) to a product of inverse PEG and the trailing‑to‑forward PE ratio (`PE / PE‑forward`). This incorporates a forward earnings improvement signal into the ranking, rewarding stocks that are both cheap on a growth basis and show earnings acceleration, which should improve the ranking’s correlation with forward returns across multiple horizons while keeping the scoring structure simple.
