**What changed & why:**  
Switched the composite score aggregation from weighted harmonic mean to weighted geometric mean. Geometric mean penalizes weak sub‑scores more sharply and better captures multiplicative interactions between factors (like valuation * quality). This often yields ranking signals that align more strongly with future returns, especially when some sub‑factors are noisy or neutered.
