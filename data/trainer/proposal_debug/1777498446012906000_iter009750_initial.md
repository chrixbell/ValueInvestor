The broken `_compute_quality_raw` method (which referenced an undefined `pe_safe`) and the cross‑sectional percentile logic are removed entirely. Instead, the quality sub‑score is computed directly as a weighted sum of several well‑established profitability, efficiency, and liquidity metrics—ROE, gross/net margins, debt‑to‑equity, current ratio, ROA, and operating cash flow yield. This eliminates the bug, avoids the failed “repair” theme, and provides a diversified, fundamental‑based quality signal that is computed per‑stock without needing cross‑sectional ranking. The change is focused on the quality module, leaving value and growth untouched, and preserves the existing class interface.
