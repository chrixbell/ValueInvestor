**What changed & why:**  
The `_compute_growth_raw` method now combines inverse PEG with the trailing-to-forward P/E improvement ratio (pe/pe_forward) into a single geometric mean when both are available, falling back to the individually available metric. This gives the cross-sectional growth ranking a second signal (forward P/E improvement) that is already known to be informative, while expanding coverage to stocks missing PEG but having forward P/E data.
