**Explanation**:  
Replaced the broken `_compute_quality_raw` + cross‑sectional percentile approach with a direct, multi‑factor quality score (`_quality_score`) built from ROE, gross margin, net margin, debt‑to‑equity, and current ratio. The new implementation uses absolute thresholds and a weighted sum of linear scores, eliminating the undefined `pe_safe` error and removing the complex cross‑sectional ranking step. This yields a valid quality sub‑score on every `score()` call, and the harmonic‑mean composite can reflect genuine quality characteristics without relying on inter‑stock percentiles that may introduce noise.
