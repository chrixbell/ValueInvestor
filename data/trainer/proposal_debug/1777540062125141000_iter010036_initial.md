**What changed & why:** Removed trailing‑PE from the quality raw metric (`(ROE × gross_margin) / ((debt+1) × PE)` → `(ROE × gross_margin) / (debt+1)`) so that the quality sub‑score reflects pure profitability and leverage, eliminating the double‑counting of valuation that was already captured by the value sub‑score.
