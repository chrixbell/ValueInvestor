**What changed & why**:  
Replaced the `FCF_YIELD_ROE` interaction term (free cash flow yield multiplied by ROE) with a pure free‑cash‑flow yield (FCF / market cap). This simplifies the factor and reduces reliance on ROE, which has already been explored in multiple failed experiments. Free cash flow yield is a clean value/quality signal that may better capture a company’s ability to generate cash after capex, potentially improving rank correlation with forward returns.
