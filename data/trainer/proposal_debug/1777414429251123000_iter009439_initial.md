**Explanation:**  

I replaced the broken cross‑sectional quality scoring (which crashed because of an undefined `pe_safe` variable) with a direct linear score of the raw profitability‑leverage metric. The quality sub‑score is now computed in `score()` using `ROE × gross_margin / (max(debt_to_equity, 0.01) + 1)` and mapped to 0–100 linearly, with cutoff defaults. This removes the bug, eliminates the unstable percentiles that likely added noise, and gives `score()` a valid quality score on the first pass—allowing the composite to be calculated once instead of being recomputed in `rank()`. The change focuses on making the quality signal monotonic and interpretable, which should improve rank correlation with forward returns.
