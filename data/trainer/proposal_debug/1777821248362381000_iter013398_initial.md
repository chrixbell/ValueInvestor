**What changed & why:**  
The growth raw metric used for cross‑sectional ranking now incorporates trailing‑to‑forward PE improvement alongside inverse PEG. Previously the raw only used `1/PEG`, so stocks without PEG data (or with weak PEG but strong forward PE improvement) were penalised in the percentile ranking even though the absolute growth score already rewarded them. By folding in `pe / pe_forward` (capped at 5.0) we make the cross‑sectional sort more consistent with the absolute growth signal, especially for the many stocks that lack a PEG ratio. This should improve ranking quality across longer horizons without disrupting the short‑term structure.
