**What changed & why:**  
The growth raw metric (used for cross‑sectional percentile ranking) now multiplies the inverse PEG by the forward‑PE improvement ratio (trailing PE / forward PE, capped between 0.5 and 2.0). This rewards stocks where the trailing PE is higher than the forward PE, indicating expected earnings growth, while preserving the original behavior when PE data is missing. The change is non‑monotonic across stocks, so it can alter the final growth‑score ordering and improve predictive power across horizons.
