**What changed & why:**  
The growth raw metric (used exclusively for cross‑sectional percentile ranking) now multiplies the inverse PEG by the forward‑PE improvement ratio (trailing PE / forward PE, capped between 0.5 and 2.0). This rewards stocks where trailing PE exceeds forward PE (indicating anticipated earnings growth) while preserving original behaviour when PE data is missing, and the non‑monotonic combination can reorder growth scores and improve return prediction across multiple horizons.
