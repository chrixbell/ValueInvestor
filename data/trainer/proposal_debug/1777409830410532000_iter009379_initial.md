**Explanation:**  
Replaced the placeholder quality score (and the broken cross‑sectional raw metric that referenced an undefined `pe_safe`) with a direct multi‑factor quality sub‑score. The new `_quality_score` combines five profitability/liquidity/leverage factors (ROE, ROA, gross margin, current ratio, debt‑to‑equity) via equal weights, each scored linearly or logarithmically. This eliminates the noisy and bug‑prone cross‑sectional step, giving every stock a stable, fundamental quality measure that should correlate better with forward returns.
