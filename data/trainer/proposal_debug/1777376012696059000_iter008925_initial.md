### Explanation
Added a new sub-factor: **free cash flow to total assets** (cash return on assets) to the value score.  
It measures how efficiently a company converts its assets into free cash flow, providing a balance‑sheet‑based yield metric that is independent of market capitalization. This may improve rank correlation with forward returns by capturing cash‑generation quality often overlooked by market‑cap‑based yields.
